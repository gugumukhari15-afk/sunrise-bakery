About Artisan Bakehouse

Our story, values and the people who make the magic happen

Our Story

Founded in 2018 in the heart of johannesburg, Artisan Bakehouse began as a small family bakery with a simple dream: to bring authentic, handcrafted bread and pastries back to the community.

What started with a single wood-fired oven and a passion for traditional baking techniques has grown into a beloved local institution. We still bake the same way – slowly, carefully, and with respect for every ingredient.

Mission & Vision

Mission: To create exceptional baked goods that nourish both body and community, using sustainable practices and supporting local producers.

Vision: To become Johannesburg’s most trusted artisan bakery, known for quality, warmth and genuine hospitality.
